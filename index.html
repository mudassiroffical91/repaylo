How the payment is calculated

We use the standard amortising loan formula. Your annual rate is split into 12 monthly parts, and each payment covers that month's interest first, with the rest reducing what you owe. Early payments are mostly interest; later ones are mostly principal.

Shorter term

Higher monthly payment, much less total interest.

Lower APR

Helps most on long loans like mortgages.

Extra payments

Paying extra early saves the most, since interest is highest at the start.

Estimates exclude fees, insurance, taxes and early repayment charges.

About ClearRate
